= ASI: Requirements catalog
:toc:
:lang: en

In the app a flat is called a *home*.

== Functional requirements

[cols="1,4,1,1",options="header"]
|===
| ID | Description | Priority | Issue

| FR-01 | Create a home by giving a name, members, zones and the rotation start date. Creating needs Google sign-in | High | Implemented (https://github.com/Glinbor10/housely/issues/21[#21])
| FR-02 | The created home opens automatically on later visits | High | Implemented (https://github.com/Glinbor10/housely/issues/21[#21])
| FR-03 | Join a home with an invite code, choose who you are and pick a free color | High | Implemented (https://github.com/Glinbor10/housely/issues/22[#22])
| FR-04 | See this week's zone assignment, highlighting your own, and the zone you have next week | High | Implemented
| FR-05 | Mark a zone as done with a date, today by default and editable, and undo it | High | Implemented
| FR-06 | Automatic, fair rotation every week, with any number of members and zones | High | Implemented
| FR-07 | View the history by week, person and zone, as a list and as a month calendar | Medium | Implemented
| FR-08 | Edit zones and members without losing the history: the change applies from the next week | Medium | Implemented
| FR-09 | Button to open the Tricount expenses with a configurable link | Medium | Implemented
| FR-10 | All members see the same state in real time | High | Implemented (https://github.com/Glinbor10/housely/issues/21[#21])
| FR-11 | Recover access: the owner releases a member whose device was lost, and that member joins again with the code | High | Implemented
| FR-12 | Find your homes again from a new phone by signing in with Google, and move the homes joined without Google to an existing Google account | High | Implemented
| FR-13 | Settle a zone that nobody cleaned: done late, covered by someone, not at home, or made up with another cleaning | Medium | Implemented
| FR-14 | Shared expenses: add expenses and payments, split them, see balances and who pays whom, and reset them periodically | Medium | Implemented
| FR-15 | Each person has a color from a fixed palette, and the owner can hand the home over to a member with Google linked | Low | Implemented
| FR-16 | Keep several homes on one device and switch between them | Medium | Implemented
| FR-17 | The owner can delete a home for good, with everything it holds | Low | Implemented
| FR-18 | Share the invite code, the week's rotation, the list of pending cleanings and the expenses summary through WhatsApp and other apps | Low | Implemented
| FR-19 | An Android app (Trusted Web Activity) built by the release and attached to it | Low | Implemented
|===
